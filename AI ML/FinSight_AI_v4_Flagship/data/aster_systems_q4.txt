ASTER SYSTEMS — Q4 MANAGEMENT BRIEF
Q4 revenue was $184 million, up 18% year over year, led by cloud security subscriptions and enterprise renewals.
Gross margin improved from 61% to 64% because infrastructure utilization increased and support costs declined.
Free cash flow was $31 million and remained positive, while capital expenditure increased due to a new regional data center.
Management identified two primary risks: longer enterprise sales cycles in Europe and higher customer-acquisition costs in the mid-market segment.
The company increased research spending on automated threat detection and plans to expand channel partnerships during the next fiscal year.
