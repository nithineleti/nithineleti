MERIDIAN ENERGY — Q4 MANAGEMENT BRIEF
Q4 contracted power revenue was $226 million, up 11% year over year, while financing costs increased due to higher interest expense.
Renewable generation capacity expanded 22% after two solar projects entered commercial operation.
Operating margin was 18% and operating cash flow was $52 million.
Management expects grid-storage projects to become a larger growth driver over the next two years.
Key risks include permitting delays, equipment lead times and power-price volatility in merchant markets.
The company maintained its project-development pipeline despite the higher financing-cost environment.
