ASTER SYSTEMS - INVESTOR DAY NOTES
Management described cloud security subscriptions, enterprise renewals and channel partnerships as the primary growth pillars for the next fiscal year.
The automated threat-detection program is expected to improve product differentiation and increase enterprise attach rates.
Infrastructure utilization and support automation remain the main operational levers for protecting gross margin as the company scales.
Capital expenditure is elevated because of a new regional data center, but management expects the investment to support future subscription capacity.
