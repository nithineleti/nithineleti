NORTHSTAR RETAIL — Q4 MANAGEMENT BRIEF
Q4 revenue was $410 million. Digital sales increased 12%, while store revenue was approximately flat.
Gross margin was 37% and free cash flow was $44 million.
Loyalty-program members produced higher repeat-purchase rates and lower promotion dependency.
Inventory days rose because of slower apparel demand, creating markdown risk for the next quarter.
Management expects logistics automation and private-label growth to support margin improvement.
The main near-term risks are inventory normalization, freight volatility and weaker discretionary spending.
