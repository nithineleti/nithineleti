# v3 -> v4 Upgrade Summary

FinSight v4 turns the previous advanced RAG demo into a broader flagship financial intelligence workflow.

Major additions:

- Orchestrated multi-agent research pipeline with observable execution trace.
- Persisted financial knowledge graph and GraphRAG retrieval mode.
- XBRL/companyfacts normalization and ratio analytics.
- Earnings-call management/analyst intelligence.
- Financial event/news classification and impact signals.
- Multimodal PDF/table inspection and OCR adapter status.
- Expanded forecasting with Gradient Boosting, Random Forest, optional XGBoost/LightGBM and ensemble trend models.
- Watchlists, alerts and portfolio concentration/P&L analytics.
- PDF/Markdown research report export.
- Advanced RAG evaluation: Precision/Recall@5, MRR, NDCG, citation precision/recall and faithfulness proxy.
- Workspace/RBAC data model and stronger prompt-injection guardrails.
- Redesigned single-page research dashboard optimized for an interview demo.
- Prometheus/Grafana and MinIO deployment profiles.
- 50+ capability registry with explicit status of offline, optional and external integrations.
