# Demo Guide
1. Open the dashboard; **Scratch** loads automatically.
2. Point out the green localization box, confidence and severity.
3. Click **Normal**, **Surface spot**, and **Edge chip** to show different outputs.
4. Upload any image to demonstrate the inference API.
5. Explain the production path: trained YOLO/PyTorch model, versioned weights, GPU serving, batch inspection, camera ingestion and drift monitoring.
